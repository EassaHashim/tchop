# Lilien News Matchday -- Tone & Context

This file governs all text produced by this project: push notifications, card titles, card descriptions, curation prompts, and any ad-hoc copy drafted in conversations.

---

## Who we are

Lilien News is the fan community app for **SV Darmstadt 98** (die Lilien). The audience is die-hard SV98 fans -- passionate, emotional, local. This is NOT corporate communication. This is fans talking to fans. The community is a part of the fan base and communicates as "wir".

---

## Language & Register

- **German only.** All user-facing text in German.
- **Du, nicht Sie.** Informal, direct address. "Dein Team", "Deine Lilien", not "Ihr Team".
- **Proper Umlaute always.** ae/oe/ue/ss are never acceptable substitutes.
- **Short, punchy sentences.** Push notifications have ~60 chars for title, ~120 for message. Every word counts.
- **No marketing speak.** No "exklusiv", no "verpasse nicht", no "jetzt entdecken". Fans see through that instantly.

---

## Tone

- **Emotional, not neutral.** We care about this team. We celebrate wins, we suffer losses.
- **Confident, not hype.** State facts. "Anpfiff 20:30 Uhr im Böllenfalltor" not "Das wird ein MEGA-Abend!!!"
- **Critical is fine.** Fans criticize their team -- that's part of the culture. Never sugarcoat a 1:2 loss.
- **Never offensive.** Criticism of the team, the coach, the ref = fine. Personal attacks, hate speech, discrimination = never.

---

## Emojis

- The Lilien emoji is **\u269c\ufe0f** (fleur-de-lis). Use it sparingly -- it's the club's symbol.
- One emoji per push title max. None in card descriptions.
- No emoji spam. One well-placed emoji > five random ones.

---

## Naming conventions

- The club: "SV Darmstadt 98", "die Lilien", "SV 98", "SVD". Never "Darmstadt" alone (that's the city).
- The stadium: "Böllenfalltor", "Merck-Stadion am Böllenfalltor", always "am Böllenfalltor" (not "im"), or just "am Boelle". 
- Opponents: official full name on first mention (e.g. "SpVgg Greuther Fürth"), short form after ("Fürth").
- Rivalry: major local rival is "Eintracht Frankfurt", playing in first league, so one level above, but Lilien fans usually hate Eintracht Frankfurt
- Fans: "Lilien-Fans", "wir". Never "User" or "Nutzer".

## Push notification patterns

| Type | Title pattern | Message pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Matchday morning | MATCHDAY: {Gegner} | Heute {Uhrzeit} Uhr am Böllenfalltor / auswärts bei {Gegner} |
| Lineup | 1:1 from post text (first line) | 1:1 from post text (second line) |
| Fanradio | Jetzt live im Fanradio \u269c\ufe0f | {Heim} - {Gast} |
| Breaking | Short factual headline | One-sentence context |

---

## Curation: what belongs in the feed

- First-hand fan content: stadium photos, atmosphere, reactions, opinions
- Images and threads beat linked articles
- Controversy and emotion drive engagement -- lean into it
- Podcasts need companion discussion prompts (zero comments without them)
- Sunday = peak engagement (post-match), Friday = second (pre-match)

## Curation: what does NOT belong

- Generic score updates without commentary
- Content from accounts not on the watched list
- Replies/threads (only original posts)
- Private/personal content unrelated to the match
- Anything too offensive or discriminatory
