# tchop.io — Ideal Customer Profiles

## Overview

tchop.io serves five core ICP segments across two broad use cases:
1. **Internal communications** — employee apps and intranet replacements
2. **External communities & media** — brand communities, news apps, local publishers

---

## ICP 1: Enterprise Employee App / Internal Comms

**Who they are:** Any enterprise with 500+ employees needing to modernize internal communication, especially with deskless or distributed workforces.

**Industries:** Any (manufacturing, healthcare, retail, logistics, corporate)

**Geography:** Worldwide — North America, Europe, Asia, Australia

**Company size:** 500+ employees

**Revenue:** 5mn EUR+

**Budget:**
- Setup fee: 10–20k EUR
- Monthly licence: 999–1,499 EUR
- Per user/month: 0.75–2 EUR (scales with volume)

**Decision makers (primary):**
- VP / Head of Communications, Internal Communications
- Head of Corporate Affairs / Corporate Communications
- Communications Director, Internal Communications Specialist

**Decision makers (secondary, smaller companies):**
- Head of Marketing, CEO, COO, Managing Director, Founder
- CTO, CIO, Head of IT, Head of Procurement

**Top pain points:**
- Disconnected workforce, especially deskless workers
- Low employee engagement and morale
- Fragmented, expensive, inconsistent communication channels
- No centralized mobile-first platform
- Poor feedback loops from employees

**KPIs they own:**
- Employee engagement scores
- Internal communication effectiveness
- Employee retention rates

**Tone:** Professional, supportive, informative

**Key competitors:**
- Direct: Staffbase, Flip, Beekeeper, Haiilo, Quiply
- Indirect: Microsoft Teams/SharePoint/Viva, Confluence, legacy intranet solutions

**Sample clients:** AOK, ceramtec, Wernsing Food Group

---

## ICP 2: Brand / Startup Community Platform

**Who they are:** Consumer or B2B brands and startups that want to build an owned community outside of social media, driven by first-party data and direct customer relationships.

**Industries:** Consumer goods, retail, e-commerce, B2B brands

**Geography:** Worldwide — North America, Europe, Asia, Australia

**Company size:** 20+ employees

**Revenue:** 500mn EUR+ (or well-funded startups)

**Budget:**
- Setup fee: 10–20k EUR
- Monthly licence: 999–1,499 EUR
- Per user/month: flat MAU-based model

**Decision makers:**
- CMO, Head of Marketing, Digital Marketing Manager, Brand Manager
- Head of Community, Community Manager, Chief Community Officer (CCO)
- Head of E-commerce, Chief Digital Officer (CDO), Head of Innovation
- CEO / Founder (smaller ventures)

**Top pain points:**
- Difficulty building a loyal, engaged customer base
- Over-dependence on social media platforms (Meta, etc.)
- Fragmented customer touchpoints
- Inability to collect first-party data due to third-party platform dependency
- Technical complexity of building owned platforms

**KPIs they own:**
- Customer engagement metrics
- Customer retention / CLV
- First-party data acquisition

**Dream outcome:** A vibrant, owned community that drives loyalty, data insights, and sales.

**Tone:** Friendly, engaging, brand-focused

**Key competitors:**
- Direct: Circle, Hivebrite, Mighty Networks
- Indirect: Custom agency development

---

## ICP 3: Content Marketing / Internal Comms Agencies (Reseller / B2B2B)

**Who they are:** Agencies specializing in internal communication, digital transformation, employee engagement, employer branding, change management, or corporate publishing. They resell tchop to their enterprise clients and build services on top.

**Priority segments:**
1. Agencies specialized in internal comms, intranet, employee engagement, internal branding
2. General content marketing / corporate publishing agencies

**Geography:** Worldwide — North America, Europe, Asia, Australia

**Company size:** 10+ employees

**Revenue:** 500k EUR+

**Budget:** Same as ICP 1 — agency acts as reseller, often adds margin on top

**Decision makers:**
- Founder, CEO, COO, Managing Director
- Head of Digital, Head of Innovation, Head of Strategy, Head of Consulting
- Internal Communication Consultants

**Top pain points (agency side):**
- Lack of a technical solution to offer clients (agencies aren't tech companies)
- Need sticky solutions that keep clients from churning
- Hard to prove ROI of comms campaigns without a platform to measure on

**Top pain points (client side they solve):**
- Clients struggle to engage deskless employees
- Clients lack unified communication hub
- Low adoption of existing tools

**KPIs they care about:**
- Client communication goals and KPIs
- Employee engagement for their clients
- Own client retention

**Tone:** Technical, authoritative, forward-thinking

**Key competitors:** Same as ICP 1 (Staffbase, Flip, Beekeeper, etc.)

**Sample clients:** AOK, ceramtec

## ICP 4: Traditional News Media (App / Community)

**Who they are:** Established publishers, online media companies, and B2C/B2B news providers looking to build direct user relationships, drive subscriptions, and increase engagement beyond their existing CMS/website.

**Geography:** Worldwide — North America, Europe, Asia, Australia

**Company size:** 20+ employees

**Revenue:** 2mn EUR+

**Budget:**
- Setup fee: 5–10k EUR
- Monthly licence: 999–1,499 EUR
- Per user/month: MAU-capped flat model

**Decision makers (product):**
- Head of Product, CPO, Head of Audience Development, Head of Mobile, Head of Engagement
- Head of Paid Content, Head of Innovation, Digital Director

**Decision makers (editorial):**
- Chief Editor, Editor in Chief, News Director, Content Director

**Decision makers (management):**
- Publisher, CEO, COO, Managing Director, Founder

**Top pain points:**
- Declining traffic from social media and Google search
- Low mobile app engagement and retention
- Difficulty adding new digital products or verticals quickly
- Limited user data for personalization
- Expensive/slow custom app development

**KPIs they own:**
- User engagement metrics (MAUs)
- Subscription growth
- User retention / renewal rates

**Tone:** Professional, insightful, forward-thinking

**Key competitors:**
- Direct: Publishing frameworks (ePaper/news-focused), CMS-connected apps
- Indirect: Internal/agency custom app development

**Sample clients:** Buzz 09 & Buzz 04 (both Ruhr24), Funke Digital

---

## ICP 5: Local News Media / Hyperlocal Ventures

**Who they are:** Small local news publishers and hyperlocal digital-first news ventures, often 1–10 person operations, many started by journalists. Frequently non-profits in the US. Need a compelling news/community product without heavy tech investment.

**Geography:** Worldwide — North America, Europe, Asia, Australia

**Company size:** 1–20+ employees (can solo operators, mostly small newsrooms)

**Revenue:** 200k EUR+ (many US cases are non-profits)

**Budget:**
- Setup fee: ~2k EUR
- Monthly licence: 399–699 EUR
- Per user/month: flat fee (low MAU volumes)

**Decision makers:**
- Founder, CEO, Editor in Chief, Publisher (often all the same person)
- Head of Digital, Audience Development Manager, Product Manager

**Top pain points:**
- Declining readership and subscription revenue
- Heavy dependence on social media for distribution and reach
- No mobile app or engagement strategy
- Limited technical resources and budget
- Difficulty building and retaining a local community

**KPIs they own:**
- Subscription growth rate
- User engagement metrics
- Subscription churn

**Tone:** Professional, informative, supportive

**Key competitors:**
- Direct: Community tools (Circle, occasionally)
- Indirect: WordPress frameworks

**Sample clients:** Dortmund24

## Cross-ICP Patterns

| Dimension | Internal Comms (ICP 1+3) | External Community/Media (ICP 2+4+5) |
|---|---|---|
| Core value prop | Smart agents and automation to scale communication and community | Own your audience, reduce social media dependency |
| Primary buyer | Head of Internal Comms / HR-adjacent | CMO / Publisher / Founder |
| End user | Employees | Customers / Readers / Community members |
| Top fear | Low adoption, employee disengagement | Churn, losing audience to platforms |
| Key differentiator vs competition | Mobile-first, ease of setup, white-label | Owned platform, first-party data, community features |

---

## tchop.io Positioning Summary

tchop.io is an **AI-powered community platform which provides white-label apps for mobile and the web** helpoing organizations own their audience, streamline communication, and drive engagement — without building custom technology. It serves both internal (employee experience) and external (customer/reader facing apps) use cases across enterprises, media companies, brands, and agencies.
