## Product

**Name:** tchop.io (always lowercase, always with .io)
**Website:** https://tchop.io
**Type:** B2B SaaS, white-label mobile app and community platform
**Target markets:** Enterprise, Media/Publishing, Brands/Startups — English and German markets
**Languages:** EN + DE (German-language variant at https://tchop.io/de/)

**One-liner:**
tchop.io is the first platform where AI agents and humans work together to turn audiences into active, owned communities.

**Extended description:**
tchop.io helps small teams create modern employee and community experiences at scale — powered by AI agents, with humans always in control. AI agents help automate curation, communication, and engagement workflows, so lean teams can do more with less manual effort. Secure by design and fully GDPR compliant, tchop.io gives organizations a trusted platform to build owned communities and stronger direct relationships.

**What it does:**
- Provides white-label mobile and web platforms for internal communication and external communities — combining quality editorial experiences with social features that drive engagement, loyalty, and retention
- Helps organizations build direct, trusted relationships in owned environments — instead of relying on third-party platforms and algorithms
- Supports enterprises in creating modern employee experiences, including frontdoor intranet-style hubs where people find communication, content, and key resources in one place
- Helps media companies, publishers, brands, and membership organizations turn audiences into engaged communities they fully own
- Brings content, communication, engagement, and intelligent workflows together in one unified platform across mobile and web
- Enables lean teams to scale curation, communication, and community operations with AI agents — while humans stay fully in control
- Provides AI agents and integrations that automate workflows, personalize experiences, and support custom agent-powered use cases
- Enables fast no-code setup, so organizations can configure and launch modern employee and community experiences in days
- Delivers a turnkey, fully managed platform so teams can focus on content, communication, and community — not technical complexity
- Offers enterprise-ready, secure, and GDPR-compliant infrastructure with flexible hosting and integration options
- Delivers premium, fully branded experiences on mobile and web in the client’s own look and feel
- Supports multilingual content and flexible global rollouts across diverse teams, markets, and audiences

**Core use cases:**
1. Employee communication / internal comms app — replaces fragmented tools, reaches deskless workers
2. Brand community platform — owned audience, first-party data, reduces social media dependency
3. News / media app — publisher-branded mobile app for readers and subscribers

## Positioning

**Primary differentiator:**
The only communication framework that is built for small teams and AI agents to help them reach out to employees, stakeholders, fans, members or subscribers every day — with quality content, smart automation, white-label branding, and without any custom development required.

**Key value props:**
- Launch in weeks, not months (most clients live in 8-12 weeks)
- AI agents and integrations for smart workflows
- Fully white-labeled — branded to the organization, not tchop
- Mobile-first, deskless-friendly (no corporate email or VPN required)
- One platform for internal and external audiences
- 10x cheaper and faster than custom app or web development
- GDPR-compliant with option to use EU-souvereign hosting

**What tchop is NOT:**
- Not a general-purpose CMS
- Not an intranet-only tool
- Not a social media platform
- Not a custom app development agency
- Not a blog or newsletter platform

## Competitors

### Internal Comms (ICP 1 + 3)
- **Direct:** Staffbase, Flip, Beekeeper, Haiilo, Quiply
- **Indirect:** Microsoft Teams / SharePoint / Viva, Confluence, legacy intranets

### Brand Community (ICP 2)
- **Direct:** Circle, Hivebrite, Mighty Networks
- **Indirect:** Custom agency development

### News/Media (ICP 4 + 5)
- **Direct:** Pugpib, Twipe, ePaper/news-focused publishing frameworks, CMS-connected apps
- **Indirect:** Custom app development

**Default comparison competitors:**
- Internal comms context: **Staffbase**
- Community context: **Circle**

**Key differentiators:**
- vs. Staffbase: tchop is perfect for small teams with focus on automation and integration; faster setup; less complex, lower price
- vs. Circle/Mighty Networks: tchop supports enterprise requirements; fully white-label native app as default service
- vs. custom dev: 10x faster and cheaper to launch
