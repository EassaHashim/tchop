## Messaging guardrails / avoid

Avoid these generic or weak framings unless context explicitly requires them:

* Avoid overusing "community platform" as the default phrase for every ICP.
* Avoid "AI-powered platform" as a primary description (too generic / too feature-like).
* Avoid vague claims like "revolutionize communication" or "transform engagement."
* Avoid "use cases" in hero copy or top-level messaging.
* Avoid feature-list copy without a clear business outcome.
* Avoid positioning AI as replacing people - always reinforce human control.
* Avoid overly technical stack language in top-level marketing copy unless speaking to technical buyers.
* Avoid generic social-media-style growth language when the stronger concept is ownership, loyalty, or direct relationships.

---

## Critical rule: Never use the same message for all ICPs

Wrong approach:
Say "community platform" every time, regardless of whether the audience is HR, publishers, or brand marketing.

Right approach:

* **HR / Internal comms:** reach, accessibility, employee experience, employee engagement, smart automation, employee app, frontdoor intranet, AI agents
* **Publishers and media:** reader loyalty, user engagement, belonging, direct customer relationships, less platform dependence, control over data and content, boost for paid offerings
* **Brand communities:** loyalty, membership models, activation, owned platform
* **Smaller local publishers:** speed, efficiency, automation, feasibility, small team, own mobile app
